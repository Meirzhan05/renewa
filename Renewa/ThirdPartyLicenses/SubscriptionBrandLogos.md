# Logo.dev subscription logos

Renewa requests subscription logos from [Logo.dev](https://logo.dev) with a user-provided publishable key. No third-party brand-logo assets are bundled in the app and no secret Logo.dev key is included.

The free commercial tier requires attribution, which Renewa displays as a link in Profile while Logo.dev is enabled. Brand names and marks remain trademarks of their respective owners; their appearance does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement.
